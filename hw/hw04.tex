\noindent
\textbf{1) Polynomial Solutions.} Using a power series ansatz, show that for each $n=1,2,3,\ldots$, the following differential equations have a polynomial solution of degree $n$.

\begin{itemize}
\item[(a)] The Hermite differential equation: $y''-2xy'+2ny=0$.

\item[(b)] The Laguerre differential equation: $xy''+(1-x)y'+ny=0$.

\end{itemize}

\noindent
\textbf{2) Modified Power Series.} For each of the following ordinary differential equations, identify singular points (if any) and derive a series solution that is valid for $-1\leq x\leq 1$. Present your final solution in a simple form when possible.

\begin{itemize}
\item[(a)] $(x^2+1)y'' - xy' + y = 0$

\item[(b)] $x^2y'' + (x+1)y' - y = 0$

\item[(c)] $x^2y'' + x^2y' - 2y = 0$

\item[(d)] $(x-1)y'' - xy' + y= 0$

\end{itemize}
